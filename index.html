Xinjia Zhao

Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences

Nanopore single-molecule sensing, bioanalysis, and medical engineering.

Engineering nanopores with the PIToN model, repeatedly measuring individual molecules, and interpreting sensing mechanisms with molecular dynamics and finite-element simulation.

Research directions

Nanopore molecular recognition

PIToN-guided pore engineering for sub-dalton citrullination sensing; recognition of glycans, sialic-acid linkage isomers, and cisplatin–DNA adducts.

↗

Single-molecule manipulation

Solid-state nanopore devices, molecular ping-pong control, and repeated capture for multiple measurements of the same molecule.

↗

Mechanism and theory

Two independent modules—molecular dynamics and finite-element simulation—interpret local pore chemistry, transport, and ionic-current signatures.
